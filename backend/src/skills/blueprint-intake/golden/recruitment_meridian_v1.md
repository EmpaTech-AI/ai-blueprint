## A) Executive Summary of Current State

Meridian Talent Partners OOD is a Sofia-headquartered B2B recruitment and talent solutions firm employing 68 FTEs across three offices in Bulgaria, Romania, and Poland [Document-Backed — org chart p.1]. The firm operates four service lines (permanent placement, executive search, contract staffing, RPO) across three primary verticals (Technology & Engineering, Finance & Legal, Manufacturing & Supply Chain) and generated EUR 4,823,400 in FY2025 revenue at 71.3% gross margin and 8.7% EBITDA margin [Document-Backed — financial summary p.1-2].

The FY2026 strategic plan is built on four board-approved priorities: Speed (reduce average time-to-fill from 38 to 28 days), Scale (formalise the RPO service line targeting EUR 600,000+ revenue), Smarter Delivery (reduce consultant admin burden by 30% and lift client satisfaction from 3.83 to 4.20 out of 5.0), and Executive Search Growth (lift exec search from 18% to 30% of revenue) [Document-Backed — strategic plan p.1-2; form: strategic context]. The plan explicitly targets EUR 6,000,000 total revenue at 25% growth [Document-Backed — strategic plan p.2].

The most urgent operational constraint is candidate sourcing: the sourcing phase accounts for 22 of the 38-day average time-to-fill (58% of total TTF), driven by 6–8 hours of manual researcher effort per mandate across 35–40 active mandates monthly [Document-Backed — sales pipeline p.3; SOP p.2]. Beneath it sits a systemic constraint: zero active integrations between any systems, with all reporting compiled manually into Excel and no single source of truth for operational performance [Document-Backed — tech inventory p.2].

Meridian is at a defined inflection point: the Vincere ATS migration (40% complete, targeting July 2026 cutover) creates a narrow window to establish data governance before the new system goes live [Document-Backed — tech inventory p.1; strategic plan p.1]. Operations Director Mihailova is the internal AI champion with both technical literacy and CEO access [Document-Backed — org chart p.2]; the two Senior Partners (Ivanov in Tech & Engineering, Georgieva in Finance & Legal) are the primary cultural resistance points [Document-Backed — previous AI initiatives p.4; form: people & culture].

## C) Detected Pain Points (Prioritized)

### Pain Point 0 — Fragmented Data Infrastructure: No Integrated Single Source of Truth
<!-- pp-id: PP-CORE-00 -->

**Statement:** There are zero active integrations between any of Meridian's systems; all data exchange is manual (CSV export, copy-paste), and all operational reporting is compiled by hand into Excel — there is no data warehouse, no BI layer, and no single source of truth for operational performance. This is the structural cause beneath the client-reporting failure and the leadership-visibility gap, and it caps the value of every other improvement.

**Evidence:**
- "There are no active integrations between any of the above systems. All data exchange is manual (CSV exports, copy-paste)" [Document-Backed — tech inventory p.2]
- "Meridian has no data warehouse, BI platform, or analytics infrastructure. All reporting is produced manually from system exports compiled into Excel. There is no single source of truth for operational performance" [Document-Backed — tech inventory p.2]
- Vincere and Xero both expose documented APIs; neither is used — the raw materials exist, the integration layer does not [Document-Backed — tech inventory p.2]
- Payroll data is not integrated with Xero or any other system; consultant-specific knowledge lives in individual inboxes and personal notes, inaccessible to any system [Document-Backed — tech inventory p.1-2]

**Impact area:** Strategic / Risk / Time
**Severity:** Critical (systemic)
**Scope:** all verticals & departments · **Precedence:** P0 (foundational) · **Absorbs:** real-time pipeline-visibility candidate (tombstone in Section H)
**Impact & audit relevance:** Severity logic: breadth × dependency, not per-instance pain — it degrades every function at once and gates the value of every other pain point (a different kind of Critical from an acute, localised one; the two are reconcilable) [Inferred — appendix item 1]. Structural cause behind Pain Point 3 (reporting) and the leadership-visibility gap in Pain Point 5; gates the internal-data value of Pain Points 1–2. The audit should verify integration feasibility on the documented Vincere and Xero APIs.
**Confidence:** [Document-Backed] — every load-bearing claim is verbatim from the technology inventory

<!-- pp-id: PP-RT-01 -->

**Statement:** Candidate sourcing is entirely manual, consuming 6–8 researcher hours per mandate and accounting for 58% of total time-to-fill. At 35–40 active mandates per month, this is the single largest operational constraint on revenue growth and the primary obstacle to the 28-day TTF target.

**Evidence:**

- SOP Process 2 documents 3–4 hours for LinkedIn Boolean search + 1–2 hours for job board search + 45 min for longlist review, totalling 5–6.75 hours minimum per mandate [Document-Backed — SOP p.2]
- LinkedIn Recruiter AI suggestions rated unreliable for specialist roles; Lusha trial abandoned for CEE data quality [Document-Backed — previous AI initiatives p.1-2]
- Candidate database provides near-zero sourcing value; researchers spend under 15 minutes on database search per mandate [Document-Backed — SOP p.2; tech inventory p.2]

**Impact area:** Revenue / Time / Cost
**Severity:** Critical (acute)
**Impact & audit relevance:** Directly blocks strategic Priority 1 (38→28 days) and the revenue-per-FTE target — the single highest-leverage process in the business [Inferred — appendix item 2]. The audit should validate researcher time allocation with a short time-tracking instrument.
**Confidence:** [Document-Backed] — corroborated across financial summary, sales pipeline, SOP, tech inventory, and previous AI initiatives
